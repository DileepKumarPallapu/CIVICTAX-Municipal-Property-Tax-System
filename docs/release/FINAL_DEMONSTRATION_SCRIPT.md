# CivicTax 20-Step Live Demonstration Script

## 1. Demonstration Setup
- **Frontend URL**: `http://localhost:5173`
- **Backend API URL**: `http://localhost:3001`
- **Recommended Browser**: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Live Demonstration Flow

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                       CIVICTAX 20-STEP DEMONSTRATION FLOW                     |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

 [1. Landing Page]  -----> [2. Public Transparency] -----> [3. CiviAssist AI]
         |
         v
 [4. Citizen Login] -----> [5. Register Property]   -----> [6. Document Vault]
         |
         v
 [7. View Assessment] ---> [8. Simulated Payment]   -----> [9. QR Receipt]
         |
         v
 [10. File Grievance] ---> [11. Officer Login]      -----> [12. Verification Queue]
         |
         v
 [13. Resolve Grievance]-> [14. Admin Login]        -----> [15. Command Center]
         |
         v
 [16. GIS Intelligence]-> [17. Recovery Center]    -----> [18. Compliance Engine]
         |
         v
 [19. Forecasting Lab] --> [20. Salesforce Integration Status]
```

### Scripted Walkthrough

1. **Step 1 — Landing Page**: Open `http://localhost:5173`. Highlight the modern municipal institutional design, Midnight Navy theme, and responsive navigation.
2. **Step 2 — Public Transparency**: Click **"Public Transparency"**. Show aggregated municipal collections without individual citizen PII.
3. **Step 3 — CiviAssist AI Guide**: Open CiviAssist widget in the bottom corner. Ask *"How is commercial tax calculated?"* to show intelligent municipal query response.
4. **Step 4 — Citizen Login**: Click **Login**, choose **Citizen** (`citizen@civictax.org`).
5. **Step 5 — Property Registration**: Navigate to **Register Property**. Enter a new parcel in Zone 1, 1,500 sq ft, upload ownership deed. Submit and show unique ID.
6. **Step 6 — Digital Document Vault**: Open **Document Vault** to demonstrate categorized ownership records and certificates.
7. **Step 7 — Tax Assessment**: Open **Assessments**. Demonstrate mathematical calculation: Built-up Area $\times$ Applied Zone Rate.
8. **Step 8 — Simulated Payment**: Click **Pay Now**. Choose UPI/Card and trigger simulated checkout.
9. **Step 9 — Instant QR Receipt**: Display generated official payment receipt. Show embedded verification QR code.
10. **Step 10 — Grievance Redressal**: File a grievance regarding property dimension clarification. Show unique tracking ticket `GRV-00109`.
11. **Step 11 — Officer Login**: Switch profile to **Municipal Officer** (`officer@civictax.org`).
12. **Step 12 — Verification Queue**: Inspect the pending property registered in Step 5. Review plot dimensions and click **Verify & Certify**.
13. **Step 13 — Grievance Resolution**: Inspect citizen grievance from Step 10. Enter field survey notes and mark **Resolved**.
14. **Step 14 — Admin Portal**: Switch profile to **Administrator** (`admin@civictax.org`).
15. **Step 15 — Municipal Command Center**: Show macro revenue KPIs, collection percentages, and zone performance charts.
16. **Step 16 — GIS Parcel Intelligence**: Open GIS map. Show interactive Leaflet markers, category filters, and synthetic location disclaimers.
17. **Step 17 — Revenue Recovery Center**: Inspect arrears aging buckets (Not Due, 1-30d, 31-60d, 61-90d, >90d). Issue a formal demand notice.
18. **Step 18 — Explainable Compliance Monitor**: View automated detection rules (CMP-001 to CMP-008) and explainable evidence indicators.
19. **Step 19 — Revenue Forecasting Lab**: Demonstrate SMA, WMA, and Linear Trend forecasts with historical observation checks.
20. **Step 20 — Salesforce Architecture & Traceability**: Open **Salesforce Integration** tab. Show staged Apex classes, custom objects, and External Client App PKCE configuration.
