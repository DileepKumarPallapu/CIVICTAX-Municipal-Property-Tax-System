# CivicTax 3.0 Department Dashboard Specifications & KPIs

## 1. Dashboard Specialization Principles

To avoid repetitive or generic layouts, every CivicTax 3.0 department workspace features customized Key Performance Indicator (KPI) cards and operational widgets derived strictly from actual database records.

---

## 2. Department Dashboard Configurations

### 2.1 Municipal Administration (Command Center)
- **KPI 1**: Total Registered Municipal Properties (City-Wide Count).
- **KPI 2**: Cumulative Assessed Property Tax (₹).
- **KPI 3**: Total Revenue Collected & Cleared (₹).
- **KPI 4**: Overall City-Wide Collection Efficiency Rate (%).
- **Widgets**: Zonal Revenue Distribution bar charts, Category revenue split pie charts, and Live System Audit Ticker.

### 2.2 Property Registration Department
- **KPI 1**: Total Ingested Applications (This Month).
- **KPI 2**: Applications Pending Initial Review.
- **KPI 3**: Successfully Registered Parcels.
- **KPI 4**: Rejected / Duplicate Detected Applications.
- **Widgets**: Recent parcel intake feed, Duplicate property reference alerts.

### 2.3 Property Verification Department
- **KPI 1**: Inspections Assigned to Current Officer.
- **KPI 2**: Pending Physical Field Surveys.
- **KPI 3**: Verifications Cleared & Certified (This Week).
- **KPI 4**: Rejection Rate with Dimensional Discrepancies.
- **Widgets**: Priority inspection route map, Document preview docket.

### 2.4 Tax Assessment Department
- **KPI 1**: Annual Demand Statements Generated.
- **KPI 2**: Assessments Awaiting Sanction.
- **KPI 3**: Average Assessed Base Value per Zone.
- **KPI 4**: Pending Reassessment Requests.
- **Widgets**: Tax rate matrix status, Fiscal year assessment timeline.

### 2.5 Revenue & Treasury Department
- **KPI 1**: Today's Collections (₹).
- **KPI 2**: Current Month Cumulative Collection (₹).
- **KPI 3**: Total Outstanding Municipal Arrears (₹).
- **KPI 4**: Delinquent Cases ($>90$ Days Overdue).
- **Widgets**: Payment method breakdown (UPI, Net Banking, Card, POS), Daily reconciliation queue.

### 2.6 Grievance Redressal Department
- **KPI 1**: Total Active Citizen Complaints.
- **KPI 2**: Cases Assigned to Current Officer.
- **KPI 3**: Complaints Approaching SLA Breach ($< 48$ hours remaining).
- **KPI 4**: Average Redressal Turnaround Time (Days).
- **Widgets**: Grievance category breakdown, Urgent grievance escalation docket.

### 2.7 Reassessment & Appeals Department
- **KPI 1**: Active Citizen Reassessment Appeals.
- **KPI 2**: Cases Under Technical Survey Review.
- **KPI 3**: Cases Awaiting Financial Sanction.
- **KPI 4**: Average Approved Value Deviation (%).
- **Widgets**: Proposed vs Current dimension comparison table.

### 2.8 Zonal Management Department
- **KPI 1**: Total Zone Properties.
- **KPI 2**: Verified Parcel Ratio (%).
- **KPI 3**: Zonal Collection Efficiency vs Target (%).
- **KPI 4**: Active Field Inspectors Deployed.
- **Widgets**: Leaflet GIS parcel heatmap, Ward-level performance ranking.

### 2.9 Citizen Self-Service Portal
- **KPI 1**: Registered Properties Owned.
- **KPI 2**: Outstanding Municipal Tax Dues (₹).
- **KPI 3**: Recent Payments Completed (This Year).
- **KPI 4**: Open Citizen Service Requests.
- **Widgets**: Quick pay button, Instant QR receipt downloader, Property card list.

### 2.10 Audit, Compliance & Telemetry Department
- **KPI 1**: Total Audited Financial Transactions.
- **KPI 2**: Active Rule-Based Compliance Alerts (CMP-001 - CMP-008).
- **KPI 3**: System Exceptions / Anomalies Logged.
- **KPI 4**: Statutory Audit Reports Generated.
- **Widgets**: Live audit log stream, CSV export command station.
